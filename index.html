About Me

A little about me

My journey in software development has primarily focused on the backend, where I've worked with server-side logic, APIs, databases, and the different pieces that make an application work behind the scenes.


While backend development has been my main focus, I've always been interested in understanding the bigger picture of web development. I'm currently expanding my knowledge in frontend development, learning how to build responsive layouts, interactive interfaces, and user-friendly experiences using modern web technologies.


I'm constantly learning, experimenting, and challenging myself to become a better developer—one project at a time.

As I continue learning and gaining hands-on experience on the frontend, my goal is to become a well-rounded Full-Stack Developer who can work across both the frontend and backend of an application. I enjoy solving problems, exploring new technologies, and turning ideas into applications that are not only functional but also intuitive and enjoyable to use.
